# Code of Conduct

Orbit welcomes people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and observable behavior, not personal attributes.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Make room for newcomers and explain project-specific context.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, sustained disruption, or retaliation are not tolerated in project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

For a private conduct report, contact the repository owner through their GitHub
profile and include links or screenshots. Do not use a public issue when doing
so. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation
against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone is representing Orbit.
